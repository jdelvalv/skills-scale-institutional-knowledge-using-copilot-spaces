## OctoAcme Project Management Approach

OctoAcme implements a structured, five-phase project lifecycle centered on customer value, iterative delivery, and data-informed decisions. Our approach defines clear roles and responsibilities, maintains transparent risk management, and emphasizes psychological safety and continuous learning.

### The OctoAcme Project Lifecycle

OctoAcme follows these five core phases:

1. **Initiation** — Validate business need, align stakeholders, and confirm success metrics through a lightweight Project One-pager that defines the problem, objective, and key deliverables.

2. **Planning** — Break approved work into shippable increments, identify dependencies and risks, estimate scope, and define clear acceptance criteria and release milestones.

3. **Execution & Tracking** — Build, test, and track progress using project boards with standardized workflows, daily standups, and small pull requests with automated testing and required reviews.

4. **Release & Deployment** — Deploy to production with confidence using pre-release checklists, smoke tests, rollback plans, and post-deployment verification.

5. **Retrospective & Continuous Improvement** — Capture learnings after each sprint or milestone, convert insights into actionable improvements with clear owners and timelines, and measure the impact of changes.

### Key Principles

- **Customer-first** — Prioritize customer value and usability
- **Iterative delivery** — Ship small, testable increments
- **Clear ownership** — Each project has named Project Manager (PM) and Product Lead
- **Data-informed** — Measure impact and iterate based on evidence
- **Psychological safety** — Encourage feedback and learning

### Core Roles

- **Project Manager (PM):** Coordinates delivery, manages schedules, risks, and communications
- **Product Manager (PdM):** Defines outcomes, prioritizes backlog, and measures success
- **Developers:** Implement features, collaborate on design and testability
- **QA/Testing:** Validates quality and acceptance criteria
- **Stakeholders:** Provide inputs and approvals

### Quality & Risk Management

Throughout all phases, OctoAcme maintains:
- **Quality Standards:** Unit tests, integration tests, end-to-end smoke tests, and security scanning in CI/CD
- **Risk Management:** A risk register identifies, assesses, and mitigates threats with structured escalation (team-level → PM → Product Lead → Sponsor)
- **Communication Cadence:** Weekly syncs between PM and PdM, twice-weekly standups, monthly stakeholder updates, and ad-hoc escalations as needed
