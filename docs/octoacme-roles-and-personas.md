# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA/Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA/Testing Leads own the quality assurance strategy and ensure that features meet acceptance criteria and quality standards before release. They partner with developers and product managers to define testability requirements and validate customer value.

### Responsibilities
- Develop and maintain test plans and QA strategies
- Define acceptance criteria and Definition of Done from a quality perspective
- Execute manual testing and oversee automated test coverage
- Identify and log defects with clear reproduction steps
- Collaborate on test automation strategy and CI/CD quality gates
- Validate releases in staging before production deployment

### Goals
- Ensure every release meets quality standards and acceptance criteria
- Build confidence in product reliability and user experience
- Reduce post-release defects and customer impact

### Typical Communication
- Sprint planning and refinement sessions
- Daily standups (when critical QA work is in progress)
- Test case documentation and defect reports
- Pre-release quality sign-offs

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **Developers**: Collaborate on test automation, acceptance criteria clarity, and defect resolution
- **Product Managers**: Validate feature acceptance criteria and usability requirements
- **Project Managers**: Provide quality status updates and manage QA timelines and dependencies

---

## Technical Lead/Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Leads guide technical direction, review architectural decisions, and help identify technical risks. They ensure solutions are scalable, maintainable, and aligned with long-term technical strategy.

### Responsibilities
- Review technical designs and architecture decisions
- Identify technical risks and propose mitigation strategies
- Guide technology choices and integration approaches
- Mentor developers on code quality and best practices
- Advocate for addressing technical debt and refactoring work
- Participate in design reviews and performance optimization

### Goals
- Ensure technical quality and long-term system health
- Reduce technical risk and complexity
- Build scalable, maintainable solutions

### Typical Communication
- Technical design reviews
- Architecture decision records (ADRs)
- Code review participation
- Technical risk discussions in planning and retrospectives

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **Developers**: Provide technical guidance, mentorship, and design feedback on implementations
- **Product Managers**: Advise on technical feasibility and trade-offs for feature requests
- **Project Managers**: Escalate technical risks and dependencies that impact timelines

---

## Security/Compliance Officer

### Role Summary
Security and Compliance Officers ensure that features meet security requirements, regulatory standards, and organizational policies. They advise on threat modeling, data protection, and incident response.

### Responsibilities
- Define security requirements and acceptance criteria
- Conduct threat modeling and security reviews
- Oversee security scanning and vulnerability assessment
- Ensure compliance with regulatory and organizational standards
- Lead security incident response and post-incident reviews
- Advocate for secure-by-design practices

### Goals
- Prevent security breaches and data loss
- Ensure compliance with regulations and company policy
- Build secure, resilient systems

### Typical Communication
- Security requirements in project planning
- Threat model documentation and reviews
- Security scanning results and remediation tracking
- Incident response coordination

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **Developers**: Define security acceptance criteria and review code for vulnerabilities
- **Product Managers**: Ensure security requirements are prioritized in roadmap planning
- **Project Managers**: Manage security milestone tracking and compliance timelines

---

## UX/Design Lead

### Role Summary
UX/Design Leads define user experience and create designs that are intuitive, accessible, and aligned with product goals. They validate usability through research and user feedback.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct user research and usability testing
- Create wireframes, prototypes, and visual designs
- Define design system standards and accessibility requirements
- Validate feature usability before and after release
- Collaborate with product and engineering on design trade-offs
- Document design decisions and maintain design systems

### Goals
- Deliver intuitive, accessible user experiences
- Validate that products meet user needs
- Reduce rework due to usability issues

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews and critiques
- User research findings and recommendations
- Design specifications and component documentation
- Usability testing sessions and feedback

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **Developers**: Provide design specs, components, and design system guidance
- **Product Managers**: Validate designs against user needs and business objectives
- **Project Managers**: Coordinate design timelines and design review milestones

---

## Sponsor/Stakeholder

### Role Summary
Sponsors represent business interests, provide strategic context, and ensure that project outcomes align with organizational goals. They approve key milestones and resource allocation.

### Responsibilities
- Define business objectives and success metrics
- Provide strategic context and business constraints
- Approve project charter, scope, and key decisions
- Allocate resources and remove organizational blockers
- Communicate project status to senior leadership
- Validate that outcomes deliver expected business value

### Goals
- Ensure projects deliver business value
- Maximize ROI and stakeholder satisfaction
- Align project execution with organizational strategy

### Typical Communication
- Project initiation and kickoff
- Monthly executive updates and dashboard reviews
- Key decision approvals
- Post-release impact assessment

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **Project Managers**: Provide approvals, escalation path, and resource decisions
- **Product Managers**: Align on business objectives and priority trade-offs
- **Developers**: Communicate business context and success metrics

---

## Scrum Master/Delivery Coordinator

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters facilitate team ceremonies, remove process blockers, and help the team maintain sustainable velocity. They serve the team and ensure continuous improvement.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate daily standups, planning, and retrospectives
- Identify and help remove blockers and dependencies
- Maintain project board and sprint artifacts
- Coach team on agile practices and continuous improvement
- Monitor team health and velocity metrics
- Coordinate with external teams on dependencies

### Goals
- Enable the team to deliver consistent, sustainable velocity
- Minimize process friction and unplanned work
- Foster a culture of continuous improvement and psychological safety

### Typical Communication
- Sprint ceremonies (daily standups, planning, retrospectives)
- Sprint board updates and status reports
- Blocker escalations and dependency coordination
- Team coaching and retrospective follow-ups

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **Developers**: Remove impediments, facilitate ceremonies, coach on agile practices
- **Product Managers**: Coordinate backlog refinement and sprint planning
- **Project Managers**: Collaborate on risk escalation and dependency management
